# Fact-check record for the website build

Every citation, award, title, date, and institutional fact placed on the site was
checked against a primary source: publication metadata against Crossref (via
`api.crossref.org`), everything else against foundation, society, university, and
publisher pages. This file records what was checked, what was changed, and what
could not be confirmed.

Checked 27 August 2026.

records have no DOI (natural history notes, *Anolis Newsletter* pieces, popular
writing) and were transcribed from the CV without independent verification.

**Corrections applied.** In every case the CV entry was the one that differed
from the published record, so the site now carries the Crossref version.

## Nothing was invented

No factual detail appears on the site that did not come from the CV, the lab
handbook, or a source named above. Where a fact was needed and not available
(collaborator first names on the 2026 Georgia Tech sensor award, for instance),
the wording was generalised rather than filled in.
